# Swirlo – Backend (Node.js & Express API)

This repository contains the backend of Swirlo, responsible for authentication, business logic, payments, order management, and user order history.

---

## Features

- **Menu Discovery:** Browse cafe products by category with search and filtering options.
- **Secure Authentication:** Email and Google sign-in with email account activation through verification links.
- **Session Security:** HTTP-only cookies for secure authentication and session management.
- **Shopping Cart:** Add products to the cart, update quantities, and manage selected items before checkout.
- **Flexible Payments:** Stripe online payments alongside Cash on Delivery.
- **Order Management:** Order history and email confirmations throughout the purchasing process.
- **Product Reviews:** Customers can share reviews and ratings for purchased products.
- **Coupons & Discounts:** Apply coupon codes and discounts during checkout.

---

## Backend Tech Stack

* **Node.js**
* **Express.js**
* **MongoDB** (Mongoose)
